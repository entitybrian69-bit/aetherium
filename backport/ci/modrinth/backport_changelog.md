Aetherium Backport 1.1.0 for Minecraft 1.12.2 (Forge): the main mod's Quality of life tab and its tuning options, brought to 1.12.2.

**New Quality of life tab** (everything off by default, client-side only, works on any server):
- Accurate placement: while you hold Use and move, a block goes down as soon as the crosshair reaches a new spot.
- Inventory: scroll over a stack to move one item at a time, Shift-drag to shift-click every stack you pass, and a Sort button (plus an optional Sort key) for chests and your inventory.
- Food info: hunger and saturation in tooltips, and your saturation drawn on the hunger bar as an AppleSkin-style gold outline.
- Waypoints with beams and Nether/Overworld conversion (B adds one, N opens the list), a death point, and Nether/Overworld coordinates on the HUD.
- Lower fire overlay, lowered shield, floating damage and heal numbers, and a custom crosshair that turns red on mobs.
- Punchy-style hands: a hand editor with presets and live preview, Show hand with items, Show both hands, and Arms sideways, height, distance and size.
- Zoom and freelook options moved to the QoL tab.

**New tuning options** (Quality and Performance tabs):
- Low-end mode, Dynamic FPS (15 FPS in the background, 1 when minimized), a menu frame-rate cap and Hitch guard.
- Entity detail distance (name tags, shadows, armour and held items), a block-entity limit per frame, an explosion cloud budget, a redstone particle limit and toggle, and throttled map updates (up to 4 times a second instead of every frame).
- Leaves mode (Fast or Fancy whatever the graphics setting), leaf culling, lighter rain and snow, and toggles for enchantment glint and End crystal beams.

Requires MixinBooter.
