First release of Aetherium Backport for Minecraft 1.12.2 (Forge).

- Aetherium terrain renderer (on by default) with automatic fallback to vanilla.
- Animated video settings screen with six tabs, light and dark themes and hover descriptions.
- Smooth chunk uploads, frame pacing, entity and block-entity distance culling.
- Dynamic lights, zoom, freelook, and fog, sky, cloud, weather and particle controls.
- Requires MixinBooter.
